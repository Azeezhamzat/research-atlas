# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

Research Atlas is built to support collective intelligence. We are committed to a welcoming, respectful, and harassment-free community for everyone, regardless of background, identity, experience, education, or level of technical expertise.

## Expected behaviour

- Be respectful, patient, and constructive.
- Critique ideas and implementations, not people.
- State assumptions and evidence clearly, especially when discussing research claims.
- Welcome questions and make space for contributors who are still learning.
- Respect privacy, consent, attribution, licences, and research ethics.
- Accept responsibility, apologise when appropriate, and repair harm where possible.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, intimidation, discrimination, or demeaning language.
- Personal attacks, trolling, threats, or unwanted sexual attention.
- Publishing another person's private information without explicit permission.
- Knowingly misrepresenting evidence, authorship, provenance, or project decisions.
- Any conduct that would reasonably make participation unsafe or unwelcome.

## Scope

This code applies in project spaces and whenever someone is publicly representing Research Atlas, including issues, pull requests, reviews, discussions, events, and other community channels.

## Reporting and enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to a project maintainer using the contact method on the maintainer's GitHub profile. Do not disclose sensitive conduct reports in a public issue. Reports will be reviewed promptly, confidentially, and as impartially as possible. Maintainers may edit or remove contributions and may warn, temporarily restrict, or permanently exclude participants whose behaviour violates this code.

Retaliation against anyone who raises a good-faith concern is prohibited.
